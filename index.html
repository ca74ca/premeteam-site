New to HYROX? Here's the whole race in 30 seconds.

HYROX is an indoor fitness race that's the same everywhere in the world. You run 1 km, then do one workout "station", and you repeat that 8 times. That's 8 km of running in total plus 8 stations, always in this order:

# Station What you do (Open division)
1 SkiErg 1,000 m on a standing ski machine (pull two handles down)
2 Sled Push Push a weighted sled 50 m
3 Sled Pull Pull a weighted sled 50 m with a rope
4 Burpee Broad Jumps Burpee, jump forward, repeat for 80 m
5 Row 1,000 m on a rowing machine
6 Farmers Carry Carry two kettlebells 200 m
7 Sandbag Lunges Lunge 100 m with a sandbag on your shoulders
8 Wall Balls 100 squats, throwing a medicine ball to a target

Plain-English notes

  • Divisions: Open is the standard race and the one most first-timers enter. Pro uses heavier weights. Doubles lets you race with a partner: you run together and split the station work however you like. Relay splits the race between a team of four.
  • Roxzone: the transition area where you move between running and each station. Moving through it quickly is free time.
  • You can stop, rest or walk whenever you need to, and plenty of people walk parts of their first race.
  • You must be at least 16 years old on race day.

Source: hyrox.com and the official HYROX Singles Rulebook, Season 26/27.

FAQ for beginners

1. I've never done HYROX or worked out much. Can I really do this?

Yes. This is who we built it for. Every session has an easier option, and the first weeks focus on building a base: easy running, basic strength and learning the stations. You'll start where you are.

2. Do I have to run the whole 8 km?

No. Walking is allowed, and many first-timers walk parts of the runs, especially later in the race. We'll train you to run more of it than you think, at a pace you can hold.

3. How long until I'm ready for my first race?

It depends on where you're starting from. Many beginners pick a race 3–6 months away. The 4-Week Program builds your base; coaching can map out the whole road to race day. Pick a race date first. Having a deadline helps.

4. How much time do I need each week?

Plan on 4–5 sessions a week, about 45–60 minutes each. If your week blows up, each plan shows you which sessions matter most.

5. Do I need a gym with HYROX equipment?

You'll need access to a wall ball, sled, SkiErg, rower and dumbbells. The best option is to train at a gym that has this equipment, such as a HYROX Training Club or a CrossFit gym. Many commercial gyms now carry rowers and sleds as well.

6. Which division should I sign up for?

Most first-timers enter Open. If you'd like company, Doubles is a great first race: you run together and split the station work with a partner.

7. What if I buy the program and it isn't right for me?

All sales are final. We recommend starting with the free Week 1 Starter Plan to get a feel for the training before purchasing the full program.
