Your first week of HYROX training

Welcome! This is your first week training with Delta Zone System. These sessions are designed for someone who is new to working out or getting back into fitness after a break. You'll learn the basics of pacing, simple strength movements, and get your first taste of HYROX stations.

What you'll need: Comfortable shoes for walking/jogging • Light dumbbells (5-15 lb / 2-7 kg) • Access to a rower or SkiErg (optional—substitutes provided) • A wall and a medicine ball (optional—substitutes provided)

The RPE Scale (How hard to work):
• RPE 3-4 = Easy, can chat comfortably
• RPE 5-6 = Working, can talk in short sentences
• RPE 6-7 = Moderately hard, breathing harder but sustainable
• RPE 8+ = Hard (not used this week)

This week, nothing should feel harder than RPE 6-7. If it does, slow down, take breaks, or use the easier options.
SESSION 1: Easy Run/Walk + Mobility (30 min)

Goal: Build your aerobic base with easy movement.

Warm-up (5 min)

Walk at a comfortable pace

Main Set (20 min)

10 rounds of:

  • 1 min: Easy jog (or brisk walk if jogging isn't comfortable yet)
  • 1 min: Walk

RPE: 4-5 — You should be able to talk the whole time

Cool-down (5 min)

Slow walk • Gentle stretches: calves, hip flexors, hamstrings (hold each 20-30 sec)

Note: If 1 minute of jogging is too much, start with 30 seconds jog / 90 seconds walk. The goal is to move continuously for 20 minutes, not to go fast.
SESSION 2: Introduction to Strength (35 min)

Goal: Learn basic strength movements that build HYROX readiness.

Warm-up (5 min)

10 arm circles each direction • 10 air squats • 10 walking lunges • 5 inchworms

Main Set (25 min)

Complete 3 rounds, resting 90 seconds between rounds:

  • Goblet Squats — 10 reps
    RPE: 5-6 • Easier: Bodyweight squats to a chair
  • Push-ups — 8 reps
    RPE: 6 • Easier: Hands on a bench or counter
  • Bent-Over Dumbbell Rows — 8 reps each arm
    RPE: 5-6
  • Reverse Lunges — 8 reps each leg
    RPE: 5 • Easier: Hold a wall for balance
  • Plank Hold — 20-30 seconds
    RPE: 6 • Easier: Plank on your knees

Cool-down (5 min)

Walk slowly for 2 minutes • Stretch: hip flexors, chest, shoulders

Note: Focus on smooth, controlled movement. Rest as needed between exercises.
SESSION 3: Rest Day or Easy Walk

Goal: Let your body recover.

Option 1: Complete Rest — Take the day off completely

Option 2: Easy 20-Minute Walk — Very gentle walk at conversation pace (RPE 3)

Think of this as active recovery, not a workout.
SESSION 4: Station Introduction (40 min)

Goal: Get your first feel for three HYROX stations.

Warm-up (8 min)

5 min easy walk or bike • 10 air squats • 10 small two-foot jumps • 5 slow inchworms

Main Set (28 min)

Complete 4 rounds with 90 seconds rest between rounds:

  • Rowing — 2 minutes easy
    Focus on smooth rhythm, not power • Stroke rate: 20-22 strokes per minute
    RPE: 5-6 • No rower? 2 min bike or assault bike
  • Wall Balls — 15 reps
    Hold medicine ball (6-10 lb) at chest • Squat down • Stand up and throw to target ~8 feet high
    RPE: 6 • No ball? Bodyweight squats + overhead press with light dumbbell
  • SkiErg — 90 seconds easy
    Stand tall, pull handles down using your core and arms • Don't rush the return
    RPE: 5 • No SkiErg? 90 sec jumping jacks or high knees at easy pace
  • Easy Walk or Jog — 2 minutes
    Recover at conversation pace • RPE: 4

Cool-down (4 min)

Walk 2 minutes • Stretch: quads, shoulders, hamstrings

Note: This is your introduction to HYROX stations. Don't worry about speed or weight—just learn the movements and how they feel when you're already a little tired.
